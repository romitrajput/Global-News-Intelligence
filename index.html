Install on your phone

Install it so it opens like an app, with no browser bar.

  1. In Chrome, tap the three-dot menu.
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. After installing, sharing text from Telegram may list this app as a target.

How items are sorted

Items you paste or upload are sorted by keyword rules on your phone, not AI, so tap an item to correct it. Live stories and the AI briefing come from the Phase 2 pipeline, which reads your Telegram, X and RSS sources and summarises them with Claude.

Duplicates are merged into one item that lists every source. Items that cover the same story are linked as related.

PDF text reading and PDF export load a small library from the internet the first time, then work offline.
